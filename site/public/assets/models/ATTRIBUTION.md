# 3D Asset Attributions (`site/public/assets/models/`)

The 3D showroom GLB assets in this directory are derived from commercial-safe open-licensed models sourced via Hugging Face (`codepawl/rocketbox-avatars-glb`, `codepawl/rocketbox-motions-glb`, and `codepawl/objaverse-clean-glb`) and refined, welded, subdivided, animated, and WebP-optimized in Blender 4.0.2 (`mcp-for-blender`).
